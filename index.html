Welcome to Jodhpur Air Quality Portal

This portal shows near real-time air quality data from different publicly available platforms, aggregated to provide a comprehensive overview of atmospheric conditions in the Jodhpur region.

Our automated systems retrieve, process, and visualize data regularly to assist researchers, students, and citizens in monitoring local air quality trends.

Disclaimer: We do not own or claim the data presented on this site. This portal only visualizes data for public understanding and research purposes. For official data, please visit respective data provider portals.
