Welcome to Jodhpur Air Quality Portal

This portal shows near real-time air quality data from different publicly available platforms, aggregated to provide a comprehensive overview of atmospheric conditions in the Jodhpur region.

Our automated systems retrieve, process, and visualize data every 24 hours to assist researchers, students, and citizens in monitoring local air quality trends.

Disclaimer: We do not own or claim the data presented on this site. This portal only visualizes the data for public understanding and research purposes. For official data, raw downloads, and authoritative reporting, please visit the official websites of the respective data providers (CPCB, NASA, etc.).
